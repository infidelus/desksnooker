Online match

Play someone on another computer or headset, with voice chat. One of you hosts and gets a five-digit room code; the other joins with it. The host's match length, reds, rules and pockets are used.

Set up the match before putting the headset on: the browser asks to use your microphone. Both players need the same build (shown under the title).

Both devices call the score, so if you're testing with two devices in the same room you'll hear each call twice, a moment apart.

    Desktop controls (mouse and keyboard)

    Mouse
    Point to aim; hold Shift and move for fine aim
    A / D
    Nudge the aim 0.1° (with Shift, 0.01°)
    W / S, Space
    Set the strength, then play the shot · or hold the left button and release
    Arrow keys
    Move the tip on the cue ball (resets to centre after each shot)
    Cue ball in hand
    Drag the white to move it within the D, or press I / J / K / L to slide it away, left, towards you or right by 1 cm (with Shift, 1 mm)
    Q / E
    Lower / raise the butt of the cue in 5° steps. With side, a raised cue swerves the ball; very steep is a massé
    V / F
    Cue view behind the ball (mouse turns the aim) · back to the free view, centred on the cue ball
    Right / middle drag
    Walk round the table · slide the view · wheel to zoom
    Z / N / G / C
    Undo · new frame · aiming guide · continue
